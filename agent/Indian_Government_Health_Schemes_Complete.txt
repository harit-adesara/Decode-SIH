====================================================================
     ALL MAJOR HEALTH-RELATED GOVERNMENT SCHEMES OF INDIA (2026)
====================================================================
Ministry: Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW), unless
otherwise noted. Compiled & fact-checked: August 2026.

This file consolidates EVERY major central government health
scheme currently active - insurance, maternal & child health,
disease control, medicine affordability, infrastructure, mental
health, and AYUSH (traditional medicine). Verify final figures on
the official portal before applying/relying on any number, as
health scheme parameters are revised frequently.

1. AYUSHMAN BHARAT - PRADHAN MANTRI JAN AROGYA YOJANA (AB PM-JAY)
####################################################################
Launched: 23 September 2018 | World's largest govt health insurance

BENEFIT:
- Rs. 5 lakh per family per year health cover for secondary &
  tertiary hospitalization - cashless, paperless, at 30,000+
  empanelled hospitals (public + private) across India
- All pre-existing diseases covered from Day 1 - NO waiting period
- Portable across India - use your card at any empanelled hospital
  in any state, not just your home state
- Since 11 Sept 2024: ALL senior citizens aged 70+ get UNIVERSAL
  coverage regardless of income (separate "Ayushman Vay Vandana
  Card") - benefiting ~6 crore seniors across 4.5 crore families

WHO IS ELIGIBLE (based on SECC 2011 database - no application/
income-proof needed if already SECC-listed):
Rural inclusion (any ONE criterion qualifies the household):
- Household without shelter / living on alms
- No adult member aged 16-59 years
- No adult male member aged 16-59 years
- Female-headed household with no adult male member
- Household with a disabled member and no able-bodied adult
- SC/ST households
- Landless households deriving major income from manual casual labour
- Primitive tribal groups, legally released bonded labourers, manual
  scavenger families

Urban inclusion (11 occupational categories): Rag-pickers, domestic
workers, street vendors/cobblers/hawkers, construction workers/
masons/plumbers/painters/welders, coolies/head-load workers,
sanitation workers, home-based artisans, washermen, security guards,
and similar informal/gig workers.

Senior citizens 70+: Universal individual cover of Rs.5 lakh
regardless of SECC status - must self-enroll via Ayushman App +
Aadhaar e-KYC if not already part of an SECC-listed family. Cannot
use both CGHS/ECHS and PM-JAY simultaneously - must choose one.

WHO IS NOT ELIGIBLE (standard non-senior category):
- Households with a regular (non-contractual) government employee
- Households with an income-tax payer
- Households owning specific assets per SECC exclusion criteria
  (motorized 4-wheeler, mechanized farm equipment, etc.)

WHAT'S NOT COVERED: Cosmetic surgery, fertility treatment (largely),
OPD-only consultation without hospital admission, drug de-addiction/
rehabilitation, and a small list of other exclusions on the official
package master list.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
1. Aadhaar card (all family members)
2. Ration card / family ID (AAY/BPL card strengthens SECC match)
3. Mobile number
4. Income certificate (only if applying under a state-specific
   extension scheme beyond core SECC list)

HOW TO CHECK ELIGIBILITY & GET THE CARD:
- Check eligibility: pmjay.gov.in -> "Am I Eligible" tool, or call
  helpline 14555 (24x7 toll-free)
- Get the card: Visit the "Ayushman Mitra" desk at any empanelled
  hospital, or nearest Common Service Centre (CSC), with Aadhaar +
  ration card; operator verifies against SECC/RSBY database and
  issues e-card after biometric/OTP authentication
- Download digital card: pmjay.gov.in -> login with registered
  mobile number
Helpline: 14555

####################################################################
2. AYUSHMAN BHARAT - HEALTH & WELLNESS CENTRES / AYUSHMAN AROGYA
   MANDIR (AB-HWCs)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: The "Ayushman Bharat" pillar that came BEFORE PM-JAY -
upgrades Sub-Centres & Primary Health Centres into comprehensive
primary care hubs (renamed "Ayushman Arogya Mandir") providing free
essential drugs, diagnostics, screening for major diseases
(hypertension, diabetes, 3 common cancers - oral, breast, cervical),
maternal & child health services, and telemedicine consultation, all
FREE OF COST, close to home.

ELIGIBILITY: Universal - any citizen can walk in and access services
free of charge; no registration or income criterion.

HOW TO ACCESS: Visit the nearest Ayushman Arogya Mandir (formerly
called Health & Wellness Centre) in your area - locatable via the
Ayushman Bharat app or local ASHA worker/ANM.

####################################################################
3. CENTRAL GOVERNMENT HEALTH SCHEME (CGHS)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Comprehensive healthcare (OPD + hospitalization + medicines
+ diagnostics) for current & retired central government employees
and their dependents through CGHS Wellness Centres and empanelled
private hospitals; cashless treatment for pensioners.

ELIGIBILITY: Serving Central Government employees (in CGHS-covered
cities), CG pensioners, sitting/former Members of Parliament,
Freedom Fighters, and their eligible dependents. Contribution is
deducted from salary/pension based on pay level.

DOCUMENTS: Government ID/service certificate, PPO (Pension Payment
Order) for pensioners, Aadhaar, dependent proof (ration card/
marriage certificate), passport photographs.

HOW TO APPLY: cghs.mohfw.gov.in - through your department's CGHS
cell (for employees) or CGHS Wellness Centre (for pensioners).


<<<SCHEME_END:3>>>
<<<SCHEME_START:4>>>
####################################################################
4. EX-SERVICEMEN CONTRIBUTORY HEALTH SCHEME (ECHS)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Cashless medical treatment for ex-servicemen (military
veterans) and their dependents at ECHS polyclinics and empanelled
hospitals - covers OPD, hospitalization, medicines.

ELIGIBILITY: All Armed Forces pensioners (Army/Navy/Air Force),
including Territorial Army pensioners and their dependents, who
have paid the one-time ECHS contribution.

DOCUMENTS: Discharge book/PPO, Aadhaar, dependent card application,
photographs.

HOW TO APPLY: Nearest ECHS Polyclinic or echs.gov.in

####################################################################
5. PRADHAN MANTRI BHARTIYA JANAUSHADHI PARIYOJANA (PMBJP) -
   "Jan Aushadhi Yojana"
####################################################################
Ministry: Chemicals & Fertilizers | Launched: 2008, revamped 2015-16

BENEFIT (for citizens): Access to over 2,110 generic medicines and
315 surgical/consumable items at Jan Aushadhi Kendras (JAKs), priced
at a maximum of 50% of the average price of the top 3 branded
medicines - typically 50-90% cheaper than branded equivalents.
Sanitary napkins ("Janaushadhi Suvidha") sold at just Re.1/pad.
Over 19,500 Jan Aushadhi Kendras operational nationwide (2026),
targeting 25,000+.

BENEFIT (to open a Jan Aushadhi Kendra - a livelihood scheme too):
- Financial assistance/incentive up to Rs. 2 lakh (reimbursed
  against purchase of medicines, up to 15% of monthly sale or
  Rs.15,000/month, whichever is lower, until Rs.2 lakh is reached)
- Additional incentive up to Rs.2 lakh for furniture/fixtures for
  Kendras in Special Category areas (NE states, Himalayan/island
  UTs, backward areas), women entrepreneurs, SC/ST, and Divyang
  (disabled) applicants
- Minimum investment to start: as low as Rs. 5,000 in some cases

ELIGIBILITY TO OPEN A KENDRA: Any Indian citizen/entity - individual
entrepreneurs, unemployed pharmacists, doctors, NGOs, trusts,
charitable institutions, private hospitals, SC/ST/women/Divyang/
ex-servicemen, Self-Help Groups, or State Governments; must employ
at least one B.Pharma/D.Pharma degree-holder as pharmacist at the
store.

DOCUMENTS (to open a Kendra): Pharmacy degree certificate of the
employed pharmacist, shop ownership/rent agreement, GST/trade
license, Aadhaar & PAN of applicant, category certificate (for
reserved-category incentive), passport photograph.

HOW TO APPLY (to open a Kendra): janaushadhi.gov.in - online
application under "Kendra" section.
HOW TO FIND A KENDRA (as a buyer): "Janaushadhi Sugam" mobile app -
locates nearest Kendra, lets you compare branded vs generic prices.

####################################################################
6. JANANI SURAKSHA YOJANA (JSY)
####################################################################
Launched: 1 April 2005 | Part of National Health Mission

BENEFIT: Cash assistance to promote institutional delivery (to
reduce maternal & neonatal mortality):
- Rural areas (Low Performing States): Rs. 1,400 per delivery
- Urban areas (Low Performing States): Rs. 1,000 per delivery
- (Amounts differ slightly for High Performing States - lower slab)
- ASHA worker also receives an incentive for facilitating
  institutional delivery & related activities

ELIGIBILITY: All pregnant women delivering in a government health
facility or an accredited private facility, with special priority
(higher focus) for BPL, SC/ST pregnant women in Low Performing States
(where institutional delivery rates are historically low).

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar card, Mother & Child Protection (MCP) card, BPL/
caste certificate (for priority processing), bank account details.

HOW TO APPLY: Through your ASHA worker/ANM (Auxiliary Nurse Midwife)
at the time of pregnancy (ANC) registration; cash benefit disbursed
at/soon after the health facility delivery.

####################################################################
7. JANANI SHISHU SURAKSHA KARYAKRAM (JSSK)
####################################################################
Launched: 1 June 2011 | Part of National Health Mission

BENEFIT: Completely FREE care for pregnant women & sick newborns at
government health facilities - free delivery (including C-section),
free drugs & consumables, free diagnostics, free blood, free diet
during hospital stay (up to 3 days for normal delivery, 7 days for
C-section), and free transport (both to the facility and back home,
plus inter-facility referral transport).

ELIGIBILITY: ALL pregnant women and sick infants (up to 1 year of
age) accessing public health institutions - universal, no income
or category restriction, no separate application needed.

HOW TO ACCESS: Directly at any government hospital/health centre at
the time of delivery or newborn illness - benefits are built into
the facility's standard service.

####################################################################
8. RASHTRIYA BAL SWASTHYA KARYAKRAM (RBSK)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Free child health screening for 4 D's - Defects at birth,
Deficiencies, Diseases, and Developmental delays including
disabilities - for children aged 0-18 years, with free treatment/
surgery/management (including free tertiary care like heart surgery,
cleft lip/palate surgery) for identified conditions, delivered
through Mobile Health Teams visiting Anganwadis & schools.

ELIGIBILITY: All children aged 0-18 years (0-6 years covered via
Anganwadi Centres, 6-18 years via government/government-aided
schools) - universal, free screening.

HOW TO ACCESS: Automatic through Anganwadi Centre/school visits by
District Early Intervention Centre (DEIC) Mobile Health Teams; no
separate application.

####################################################################
9. RASHTRIYA KISHOR SWASTHYA KARYAKRAM (RKSK)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Adolescent health programme (ages 10-19) covering
nutrition, sexual & reproductive health, mental health, substance
misuse prevention, injury & violence prevention, non-communicable
disease awareness - delivered through Peer Educators, Adolescent
Friendly Health Clinics (AFHCs), and school health programmes
including Weekly Iron & Folic Acid Supplementation (WIFS).

ELIGIBILITY: All adolescents aged 10-19 years - universal, free
access.

HOW TO ACCESS: Nearest Adolescent Friendly Health Clinic (AFHC) at
a government health facility, or through school health programmes.

####################################################################
10. MISSION INDRADHANUSH / INTENSIFIED MISSION INDRADHANUSH (IMI 4.0/5.0)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Free full immunization against 12 vaccine-preventable
diseases (Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Polio, Measles, Rubella,
severe TB, Hepatitis B, Meningitis/Pneumonia from Hib, Pneumococcal
disease, Rotavirus diarrhoea, Japanese Encephalitis in endemic
districts) for children under 2 years and pregnant women, through
intensive catch-up immunization drives in low-coverage pockets.

ELIGIBILITY: All children under 2 years of age and all pregnant
women who are unvaccinated or partially vaccinated - universal,
free of cost.

HOW TO ACCESS: Anganwadi Centre, government health sub-centre/PHC,
or during scheduled Mission Indradhanush immunization drive days
(announced periodically); track via CoWIN/UWIN portal for records.

####################################################################
11. NATIONAL TUBERCULOSIS ELIMINATION PROGRAMME (NTEP) /
    NI-KSHAY POSHAN YOJANA
####################################################################
Goal: TB-Mukt Bharat (TB elimination target advanced to 2025)

BENEFIT:
- FREE TB diagnosis (including molecular diagnostic tests) and FREE
  treatment (drugs) for all forms of TB (drug-sensitive & drug-
  resistant) at government facilities
- Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana: Rs. 1,000/month nutritional support cash
  transfer to every notified TB patient for the duration of
  treatment (via DBT to bank account)
- Ni-kshay Mitra initiative: community/corporate volunteers "adopt"
  TB patients to provide additional nutritional/diagnostic support

ELIGIBILITY: Any person diagnosed with TB (pulmonary or extra-
pulmonary) and notified in the Ni-kshay portal by a treating doctor
(government or private) - universal, no income criterion.

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar card, bank account details (for Ni-kshay Poshan
Yojana DBT), TB diagnosis/notification record.

HOW TO ACCESS: Any government TB treatment centre / DOTS centre, or
private practitioner who notifies the case on the Ni-kshay portal
(nikshay.in); nutritional support auto-processed after notification.

12. NATIONAL VIRAL HEPATITIS CONTROL PROGRAMME (NVHCP)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Free screening and FREE treatment for Hepatitis B & C
(including free antiviral drugs) at designated government treatment
centres; free Hepatitis B birth-dose vaccination for newborns.

ELIGIBILITY: Universal - any citizen can access free screening;
confirmed Hepatitis B/C patients get free treatment at government
centres.

HOW TO ACCESS: Nearest government district hospital's Hepatitis
Treatment Centre or Model Treatment Centre.

####################################################################
13. NATIONAL AIDS CONTROL PROGRAMME (NACP) - via NACO
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Free HIV testing & counselling (Integrated Counselling &
Testing Centres - ICTC), free lifelong Anti-Retroviral Therapy (ART)
for HIV-positive persons, free treatment for Sexually Transmitted
Infections (STIs), condom distribution, and targeted interventions
for high-risk groups.

ELIGIBILITY: Universal, confidential & free - any citizen can walk
into an ICTC for testing; HIV-positive individuals are enrolled at
an ART Centre for free lifelong treatment.

HOW TO ACCESS: Nearest government ICTC or ART Centre; helpline:
1097 (NACO 24x7 toll-free helpline).


<<<SCHEME_END:13>>>

####################################################################
14. PM NATIONAL DIALYSIS PROGRAMME (PMNDP)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: FREE dialysis services for poor patients suffering from
Chronic Kidney Disease/kidney failure, at government district
hospitals, under a Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model.

ELIGIBILITY: All patients requiring dialysis who approach a
government district hospital under the programme - priority to BPL
patients, though many states extend free/subsidized dialysis more
broadly.

HOW TO ACCESS: District Hospital Dialysis Centre - ask the hospital's
nephrology/dialysis unit about PMNDP enrollment.

15. PRADHAN MANTRI SURAKSHIT MATRITVA ABHIYAN (PMSMA)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: FREE, fixed-day (9th of every month) comprehensive
antenatal check-up by a specialist/doctor for pregnant women in
their 2nd/3rd trimester, at government health facilities, including
free ultrasound, blood tests, and high-risk pregnancy identification.

ELIGIBILITY: All pregnant women in their 2nd/3rd trimester -
universal, free, no application needed.

HOW TO ACCESS: Visit the designated government health facility on
the 9th of any month (or next working day if 9th is a holiday).

16. AYUSHMAN BHARAT DIGITAL MISSION (ABDM)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Voluntary digital health ecosystem - creates a unique
14-digit ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) ID/health ID linking
all your medical records digitally, enabling paperless, portable
health records shareable with any consenting doctor/hospital across
India; also links to Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Centres and
telemedicine (eSanjeevani).

ELIGIBILITY: Any Indian citizen - free, voluntary enrollment.

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar card or mobile number (for ABHA ID creation).

HOW TO APPLY: abha.abdm.gov.in or the "ABHA" mobile app - create
your Health ID in minutes using Aadhaar/mobile OTP.

17. eSANJEEVANI (National Telemedicine Service)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: FREE online doctor consultation (video/audio) from home,
including specialist consultation via the "eSanjeevani OPD" (patient-
to-doctor) service, and doctor-to-doctor teleconsultation at
Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (Hub-and-Spoke model) - reduces need to
travel for common ailments.

ELIGIBILITY: Universal - any citizen with a smartphone/internet
access; free of cost.

HOW TO ACCESS: esanjeevani.mohfw.gov.in or the eSanjeevani app -
register with mobile number, book a free e-OPD slot.

18. PRADHAN MANTRI SWASTHYA SURAKSHA YOJANA (PMSSY)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Infrastructure scheme - establishes new AIIMS-like
institutions and upgrades existing Government Medical Colleges to
create additional tertiary-care capacity and increase MBBS/PG
medical seats across India (a systemic infrastructure scheme, not
a direct individual cash/insurance benefit).

ELIGIBILITY: Patients benefit indirectly through improved access to
tertiary healthcare in their region as new/upgraded institutions
come online; medical students benefit through increased seats.

19. PRADHAN MANTRI - AYUSHMAN BHARAT HEALTH INFRASTRUCTURE MISSION
    (PM-ABHIM)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Rs. 64,180 crore mission (2021-2026) strengthening health
infrastructure at all levels - Block Public Health Units, Integrated
District Public Health Labs, Critical Care Hospital Blocks in
districts, disease surveillance systems, and pandemic preparedness
- a systemic/infrastructure scheme benefiting the public through
better-equipped hospitals in their district over time.

ELIGIBILITY: Not an individual-benefit scheme - infrastructure
becomes available to the general public in covered districts.

20. NATIONAL MENTAL HEALTH PROGRAMME (NMHP) / TELE-MANAS
####################################################################
BENEFIT: 
- Free/subsidized mental healthcare through District Mental Health
  Programme (DMHP) units at government hospitals
- Tele MANAS (Tele Mental Health Assistance and Networking Across
  States): FREE 24x7 tele-counselling helpline for mental health

HOW TO ACCESS: Tele MANAS helpline: 14416 or 1-800-891-4416

21. NATIONAL PROGRAMME FOR PREVENTION & CONTROL OF NON-COMMUNICABLE
    DISEASES (NP-NCD) - Cancer, Diabetes, CVD, Stroke
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Free population-level screening for common Non-Communicable

HOW TO ACCESS: Nearest Ayushman Arogya Mandir / District NCD Clinic
for free screening.

22. NATIONAL AYUSH MISSION (NAM)
####################################################################
Ministry: Ministry of AYUSH (separate ministry since 2014)

BENEFIT: Promotes Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and
Homoeopathy (AYUSH) systems - funds AYUSH hospitals/dispensaries
co-located with government health facilities, provides free/
subsidized AYUSH treatment, cultivation support for medicinal
plants, and AYUSH wellness centres.

ELIGIBILITY: Universal - any citizen can access AYUSH OPD/treatment
at government AYUSH hospitals/dispensaries, generally free or at
nominal cost.

HOW TO ACCESS: Nearest government AYUSH hospital/dispensary, or
AYUSH wing at a district hospital.

23. PRADHAN MANTRI JEEVAN JYOTI BIMA YOJANA (PMJJBY)
    (life insurance - included here as it's health/life-security
    related)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Life insurance cover of Rs. 2 lakh (death due to any cause)
for an annual premium of Rs. 436, auto-debited from bank account.

ELIGIBILITY: Indian citizen aged 18-50 years with a bank/post office
account; renewable yearly till age 55.

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar card, bank account, nominee details, self-
declaration of good health at first enrollment.

HOW TO APPLY: Through your bank branch/net-banking/mobile banking
app opt-in form, or post office.

24. PRADHAN MANTRI SURAKSHA BIMA YOJANA (PMSBY)
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Accidental death & disability insurance - Rs. 2 lakh for
accidental death or total permanent disability, Rs. 1 lakh for
partial disability, for a premium of just Rs. 20/year.

account; renewable annually.

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar card, bank account, nominee details.

HOW TO APPLY: Bank branch/net-banking/mobile banking app opt-in

####################################################################
25. EMPLOYEES' STATE INSURANCE (ESI) SCHEME
####################################################################
BENEFIT: Comprehensive medical care (self + family), sickness cash
benefit, maternity benefit, disablement benefit, dependents' benefit
- for organized-sector workers, through ESIC hospitals/dispensaries.

ELIGIBILITY: Employees earning gross wages up to Rs. 21,000/month
(Rs. 25,000/month for persons with disability) in ESI Act-covered
establishments (10+ employees, varies by state/sector).

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar, PAN, bank account, employer's ESI registration
number, family photograph, employment proof.

HOW TO APPLY: Registration done by the employer at esic.gov.in;
employee receives an ESI/Pehchan card.

28. PRADHAN MANTRI MATRU VANDANA YOJANA (PMMVY)
####################################################################
(Detailed earlier under Women & Child Welfare - included here again

BENEFIT: Rs. 5,000 cash incentive for first living child (paid in

####################################################################
GENERAL DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST FOR HEALTH SCHEMES
####################################################################
1. Aadhaar Card - needed for almost every scheme (all family members
   for family-based schemes like PM-JAY)
2. Ration Card / Family ID / BPL Card - determines SECC-based
   eligibility for PM-JAY, PMAY convergence, food-linked nutrition
3. Mother & Child Protection (MCP) Card - for JSY, JSSK, PMMVY,
   Mission Indradhanush tracking
4. Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar-linked) - for any cash-transfer
   component (Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana, JSY, PMMVY)
5. Mobile Number - for OTP-based registration on ABHA, e-Sanjeevani,
   Ayushman App
6. Employment/Pension Proof - for CGHS/ECHS/ESI eligibility
7. Disability Certificate - for enhanced ESI wage ceiling & other
   NCD/disability-linked health benefits

####################################################################
IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
####################################################################
- All schemes listed are officially free to access through
  government channels - never pay a middleman/tout claiming to
  "guarantee" faster treatment or approval.
- Figures (cover amounts, cash incentives, income ceilings) are
  revised periodically via Union Budgets and ministry notifications.
  Always verify current details on the official ".gov.in" portal or
  by calling the relevant helpline before relying on a number.
- If you or someone you know is in a medical emergency, go to the
  nearest hospital immediately or call 108 - do not wait to check
  scheme eligibility first; JSSK and PM-JAY (for eligible families)
  cover emergency treatment too, and hospitals are expected to treat
  emergencies regardless of documentation status.
